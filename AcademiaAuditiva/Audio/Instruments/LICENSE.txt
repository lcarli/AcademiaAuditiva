Instrument samples
==================

The samples in guitar/ and violin/ were made from the nylon-string guitar
(acoustic_guitar_nylon) and violin renderings of the FluidR3_GM SoundFont
published by the midi-js-soundfonts project, at commit
044fab8e1456bfafc5776e86dfd6bb8697149aef:

  https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/044fab8e1456bfafc5776e86dfd6bb8697149aef/FluidR3_GM

midi-js-soundfonts releases its FluidR3_GM renderings under the Creative
Commons Attribution 3.0 license:

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

Changes: scripts/build-instrument-samples.ps1 mixed every note down to mono,
set its loudness to that of the piano samples and encoded it as a 44.1 kHz,
96 kbps MP3. The violin rendering has no sound for A#6 and F#7 to B7; those
notes were made by resampling the nearest violin note below to their pitch.
